Privacy Policy

iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner · Effective November 2026

This policy describes how iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner (“the App”) handles information. Core scanning and code generation run on your device. The App does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync of your scan history. The App also uses Google advertising and Firebase services that process limited technical, usage, diagnostic, and notification-related information as described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner application. It explains what information is processed when you use features such as camera scanning, gallery import, history, sharing, downloads, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and optional notifications.

2. Information we collect or process

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be processed:

  • QR / barcode contents you scan or create — processed on-device and, if history is saved, stored locally on your device (including the full payload text). The App’s own code does not upload those contents to our servers.
  • Camera frames — used in real time to detect codes in the viewfinder. The App does not include a feature that uploads camera frames to our servers.
  • Photos you select — used on-device to decode a code from an image or to embed a logo in a custom QR design.
  • App preferences — for example theme mode and notification preference flags, stored locally.
  • Advertising-related information — such as advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, and ad interaction data processed by Google AdMob (see Advertising).
  • App activity and diagnostics — such as app instance identifiers, screen and lifecycle events, custom usage events, and crash reports processed through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (see Analytics and Crash reporting).
  • Notification delivery information — such as Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens, installation identifiers, and topic subscription state used to deliver notifications (see Notifications).

The App does not provide user registration, profiles, or cloud sync of your scan history. The App does not collect precise device location for App features.

3. Camera and QR / barcode scanning

Camera permission is requested so the App can detect QR codes and barcodes in real time. Scanning is performed on your device using the camera preview. Detected content is shown to you in the App and may be saved to local history.

We do not operate a backend that receives your live camera stream. If you open a URL or other actionable content decoded from a code, that action uses your device’s system browser or related apps (see Sharing, exports & links).

4. Photos, gallery, and image editing

With your permission, the App may access photos you choose in order to:

  • Decode a QR code or barcode from a gallery image
  • Import a logo or image for custom QR designs
  • Save or export generated QR / barcode images you create

Selected images are processed on your device for these features. The App does not include a feature that uploads your gallery photos to our servers. Photos and files are accessed or exported only for these user-requested features.

5. Locally stored data and history

Scan and generation history (including titles, full content payloads, format/type, timestamps, and optional styling metadata) is stored locally on your device. On supported Android devices, history is stored using encrypted storage provided through flutter_secure_storage, with encryption backed by the Android Keystore.

Theme preference and certain notification-related preference flags are stored locally using ordinary on-device preferences storage (not the encrypted history store).

QR and barcode contents stored in history are not uploaded to our servers by the App’s own code. You can delete individual history items or clear all history from within the App (Settings / History). Uninstalling the App typically removes app-local data according to your operating system.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob (including banner and interstitial ads). The AdMob SDK may process information such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • Ad interactions and other advertising-related information

Advertising data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in Google’s advertising and privacy documentation. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (AdMob)
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and App performance. This may include:

  • App instance identifiers and relevant device identifiers
  • App interactions, app lifecycle events, and screen events
  • Custom events such as scan completion and QR or barcode generation (for example event names and metadata such as format or type labels), without sending the raw QR or barcode payload text through analytics
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address, where applicable under Google’s practices

The App’s analytics instrumentation is not designed to transmit the full contents of scanned or generated codes to Firebase Analytics. See Firebase Privacy and Security for Google’s description of how Firebase processes data.

8. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and related diagnostic information so we can improve stability. This may include:

  • Crash reports, stack traces, and diagnostic logs
  • Device and App information associated with crash reports
  • Installation identifiers, where applicable

Additional details are described in Firebase Privacy and Security.

9. Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver optional notifications (for example feature updates or tips). When notifications are enabled, the App may process:

  • Firebase registration tokens and installation identifiers
  • Notification delivery and topic subscription, including subscription to the all_users topic used for general App notifications
  • Relevant App and device information processed by FCM for notification delivery

You can control notification preference from within the App settings (subject to your device’s permission settings). Topic subscription depends on notification preference and availability of a registration token. Notification delivery can also depend on device settings, network conditions, and Google’s messaging infrastructure.

See Firebase Privacy and Security for more information about Firebase data practices.

10. Service providers and data sharing

The App relies on the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Firebase Analytics — usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — optional notifications

Data processed by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging is handled by Google in connection with providing those App services. Advertising-related information processed through AdMob may be shared with Google and its advertising partners for advertising purposes. We do not treat every Firebase data transfer as advertising-related third-party sharing.

We do not sell your QR or barcode history. We do not operate a separate custom backend that receives your scan history.

11. Sharing, exporting, and external links

When you choose to:

  • Share text, images, or contact cards — the App uses the system share sheet; the destination app is chosen by you.
  • Download / save generated images — files may be written to Downloads, Pictures, app storage, or documents folders depending on platform and permissions.
  • Open a URL, phone number, email, SMS, or map location — the App hands off to the appropriate system app or browser.

Sharing through external apps is initiated by you. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party apps or websites you open after leaving the App.

12. Data retention and deletion

  • Local history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App (subject to OS behaviour).
  • Advertising, analytics, crash, and messaging data processed by Google services is retained according to Google’s practices for those services. The App does not provide an in-app control that deletes data already held by Google on our behalf.

If you need help with a privacy request, use the contact details below.

13. Security

We aim to handle App features responsibly and rely on platform security such as OS sandboxing. On supported Android devices, scan and generation history is stored using encrypted storage backed by the Android Keystore as described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect access to your device, and clear local history if you no longer want scanned or generated content kept on the device.

14. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts — the App has no account system.

If you have a privacy concern related to a child’s use of the App, contact us using the details below so we can review the request.
